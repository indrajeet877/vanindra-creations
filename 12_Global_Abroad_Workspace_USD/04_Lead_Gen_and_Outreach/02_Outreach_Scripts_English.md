7. "How much time can you give each week — to film, or to approve videos?"

## 9. Objection Handling

| They say | We say |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"You're overseas."** | "We are — our studio is in India, and that's what makes overnight turnaround possible. You'll still have a signed agreement, USD invoices, named people and a weekly schedule. Most of our work is async, and calls happen in your morning." |
| **"How do I know the quality will be good?"** | "You shouldn't take our word for it. We'll edit 15 seconds of one of your videos for free. Then try the 3-video pilot for $149 — if the first video doesn't match the brief after two revision rounds, we refund it in full." |
| **"The time difference worries me."** | "It actually works in your favour: you upload before you log off and review finished drafts the next morning. For live calls, we're available [9 am–12 pm ET / 9 am–1 pm UK / 2:30–5 pm Sydney]. And you'll always get a reply within one business day." |
| **"It's too expensive."** | "Totally fair. Can I ask what you're comparing it to? … If volume is the issue, Spark is 12 videos for $500. If you'd rather test first, the pilot is $149 and fully credited. And if you prepay a quarter, it's 10% off." |
| **"Freelancers on Fiverr charge $20 a video."** | "Some do great work at that price, and for one-off edits that can be enough. What clients pay us for is the system: hooks written for your audience, a consistent style, next-day delivery, a quality check before you see anything, and someone thinking about what to post next month. The pilot is the easiest way to compare." |
| **"AI tools do this for $20 a month."** | "Use them — we do too, for the first pass. AI picks moments based on patterns; it doesn't know your offer or your audience, and it gets captions and names wrong. Our editors choose clips that sell what you sell, fix every caption and make sure nothing off-brand goes out." |
| **"This seems cheap — what's the catch?"** | "No catch. Our costs are lower than a US or UK studio's, and we pass that on. The quality bar is the same — that's why we offer the pilot and the refund guarantee." |
| **"Can you guarantee views / followers?"** | "No one honestly can — platforms control distribution, and anyone promising numbers is guessing or buying fake engagement. We guarantee the work: the number of videos, the deadlines and the quality. We report on performance monthly and adjust what we make." |
| **"We had a bad experience with an agency."** | "I'm sorry — what went wrong? … That's exactly why we're month-to-month with 15 days' notice, and why you'll see a review link for every video before anything is final." |
| **"I don't have time to film."** | "Most clients batch-record once a week in about 30–45 minutes using our scripts. And if you already have long-form — podcasts, webinars, Zoom calls — we can start there with no new filming at all." |
| **"Send me some info."** | "Happy to. So I send the right thing — is it pricing, examples in your niche, or how the process works?" → send only that, then book a follow-up. |
| **"I need to think about it."** | "Of course. What would you like to think through — budget, fit or timing?" → address it → agree a specific follow-up date. |
| **"Do you need my passwords?"** | "Never. We use partner access through Meta Business Suite, YouTube channel permissions and your scheduling tool, so you can remove us with one click at any time." |
| **"Who owns the videos?"** | "You do. Once an invoice is paid, the final videos are yours to use anywhere." |
| **"Is the music licensed?"** | "Yes — we use a licensed library for anything we add, or you can add platform audio when you post." |
