## 1. Country Prioritisation

### Scoring logic
We score each market on four things:
1. **English is the default business language** (no translation, native-feeling copy works).
2. **Purchasing power / willingness to pay USD retainers** for marketing services.
3. **Short-form demand already exists** (businesses there already post Reels / TikTok / Shorts / LinkedIn video and hire editors — we don't have to educate the market).
4. **Time-zone fit** for our overnight model and for live calls in Smita Yadav's evening.

### S1 — Podcasters & Video Podcasts
- **Pain:** Record 1–4 hours a week; clips are the main discovery channel, but clipping takes hours; AI clippers pick generic moments and mangle captions; guests expect clips to share.
- **What they buy:** "X clips per episode" packages; audiograms are old news — they want 9:16 video with captions and a hook title.
- **Buying signals:** Video episodes on YouTube but few or no Shorts; Shorts posted irregularly; guests not tagged; a sponsor slot (= revenue); a "work with me" or booking link.
- **Where to find them:** Apple Podcasts & Spotify category charts (Business, Health & Fitness, Education); YouTube search "[niche] podcast" filtered to recent uploads; Listen Notes / Podchaser search; LinkedIn "host of"; X/Twitter podcast communities; podcast guest-matching platforms.
- **Opener:** Free Clip Map (3 timestamps + hook lines from their latest episode).
- **Plan fit:** Spark (12 clips ≈ 3 per weekly episode) → Momentum (5 per week + covers + platform versions).

### S2 — Health & Wellness Practitioners
- **Pain:** Need patient/client education content to build trust; no time; worry about saying the wrong thing; agencies don't understand the science.
- **What they buy:** Monthly content retainers; talking-head edits; myth-busting series; FAQ videos.
- **Buying signals:** Practitioner posts sporadically; long caption-only posts (they have ideas, no video); a "book an appointment" link; an online program or meal plan for sale.
- **Where to find them:** Instagram & TikTok searches (registered dietitian, physiotherapist, chiropractor, dentist, med spa + city); Google Maps for clinics in target cities → website → socials; LinkedIn for clinic owners; Linktree / Stan Store links.
- **Compliance note (internal):** We **do not** add claims, before/after medical results or patient testimonials on our own. The client approves every claim. For clinics, we do **not** accept footage of identifiable patients unless the client confirms written patient consent. We are not set up for US HIPAA business-associate arrangements — **confirm with a lawyer before taking any clinic that wants us to handle patient data.**
- **Plan fit:** Momentum (scripts matter here) → Full Studio for multi-location clinics.

### S3 — Coaches & Consultants
- **Pain:** Content fills their calendar, but posting is inconsistent; they film but never edit; they've tried freelancers who vanished.
- **What they buy:** Scripted talking-head content + editing; "content that books calls".
- **Buying signals:** "Book a call" / application link in bio; a $1K+ program; an active email list; occasional Reels with no captions.
- **Where to find them:** IG/TikTok with Kajabi/Stan/Calendly links in bio; Skool and Circle communities (participate — don't spam); LinkedIn; guests on podcasts in their niche.
- **Plan fit:** Momentum (scripts + 20 videos).

### S4 — B2B Founders & Executives on LinkedIn
- **Pain:** LinkedIn video is growing (to verify for their niche); ghostwriters handle text but not video; they record on Zoom/phone and footage sits unused.
- **What they buy:** Talking-head clips with captions in 4:5 / 9:16; clips from webinars, keynotes and podcast guest spots.
- **Buying signals:** Posts text 3+ times a week but little video; has appeared on podcasts (footage exists!); recently raised funding or launched a product; hiring a "content marketer".
- **Where to find them:** LinkedIn search (founder / CEO + industry, filter posts with video); LinkedIn Sales Navigator if budget allows; Substack/beehiiv newsletter authors; conference speaker pages.
- **Plan fit:** Momentum → Full Studio (when they want posting + engagement handled).

### S5 — Real Estate Agents, Teams & Mortgage Brokers
- **Pain:** Listing tours, market updates and "agent personality" content are expected; they shoot on phones; turnaround speed matters (listings move fast).
- **What they buy:** Fast listing-video edits, market-update shorts, personal-brand content.
- **Buying signals:** Active listings; IG/TikTok with raw, unedited tours; team page with multiple agents (bigger budget).
- **Where to find them:** IG/TikTok "[city] realtor"; brokerage team pages; US: Zillow / Realtor.com agent profiles; AU: realestate.com.au / Domain agent profiles; UAE: Property Finder / Bayut agent pages.
- **Plan fit:** Spark (volume, simple style) → Momentum for teams. Sell "next-day listing edits".

### S6 — E-commerce / DTC Brands
- **Pain:** Ad creative fatigue — they need many hook variants of the same UGC footage; in-house editors are overloaded.
- **What they buy:** Per-ad edit packs, hook variant packs, monthly creative retainers.
- **Buying signals:** Active video ads in the Meta Ad Library; TikTok Shop presence; job posts for "UGC editor" or "performance creative".
- **Where to find them:** Meta Ad Library; TikTok Creative Center; Shopify store discovery tools; Product Hunt / Kickstarter launches.
- **Note:** We edit; the brand supplies UGC footage (we don't supply US creators). Performance claims are off-limits — we deliver variants, they test.
- **Plan fit:** Momentum + Ad Variant add-on, or a custom quote.

### S7 — YouTubers Needing Shorts
- **Pain:** Shorts help discovery, but they don't want to spend time cutting them.
- **What they buy:** "Shorts from each long video" packages; sometimes long-form editing.
- **Buying signals:** 10K–500K subscribers with irregular or no Shorts; consistent long-form uploads.
- **Where to find them:** YouTube search; Social Blade / vidIQ to sort channels; creator Discords; X.
- **Note:** Lower budgets and price-shopping are common — keep to Spark and don't over-invest in sample edits here.

### S8 — Agencies Needing White-Label Overflow (Channel)
- **Pain:** Social media / marketing agencies win clients faster than they can hire editors.
- **What they buy:** Reliable per-video editing under their brand, with fast turnaround.
- **Buying signals:** Job posts for "short-form video editor" (LinkedIn, Indeed, Upwork); team of 2–20 people; "we create content for…" positioning.
- **Where to find them:** LinkedIn company search; agency directories (Clutch, etc.); job boards.
- **Why it matters:** One agency can send multiple accounts. Lower margin per video, but zero sales effort per end client.

## 4. Outreach Compliance Notes (General — Verify Before Scaling)

These are **general pointers, not legal advice.** Confirm with a qualified advisor before scaling cold email.

- **US (CAN-SPAM):** Commercial emails need accurate sender info, a non-deceptive subject line, a valid physical postal address, and a clear way to opt out, with opt-outs honoured promptly.
- **Canada (CASL):** Requires consent (express or, in limited cases, implied — e.g., an address conspicuously published for business with no "no solicitation" notice, and a message relevant to their role) plus sender identification and an unsubscribe mechanism. **To verify** — when unsure, use LinkedIn/DM instead.
- **UK (UK GDPR + PECR):** B2B email to corporate addresses is treated differently from email to sole traders/individuals. **To verify** — for UK sole traders (many coaches), prefer LinkedIn/DM.
- **Australia (Spam Act 2003):** Consent-based; inferred consent may apply to conspicuously published business addresses where the message relates to their role. **To verify.**
- **EU / Ireland (GDPR):** Legitimate-interest B2B outreach needs care; always offer an opt-out and keep records. **To verify.**
- **Everywhere:** Honour every "no" and "unsubscribe" immediately (`Opt_Out = Y` in the tracker, never contact again).
