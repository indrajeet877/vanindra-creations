## 3. The Entry Offer — Why "Free 15-Second Sample + $149 Pilot"

### Free 15-second sample edit (qualified leads only)
- One existing video, first 15–20 seconds, re-edited with a hook title, animated captions, 1–2 B-roll overlays, punch-ins and sound design.
- 15–20 minutes of Akarsh's time. **Hard cap: 2 per day, 10 per week.** Only Fit Score 7+.
- Delivered privately via an unlisted review link within 48 hours. Never posted publicly without written permission.

### $149 Pilot — 3 videos
- 3 finished shorts (up to 60 sec each) from the client's footage, Momentum-level style, with captions, hook text and a cover frame.
- **2 revision rounds** per video. Delivered within **5 business days** of receiving footage.
- **100% credited** toward the first month of any plan if they sign within **14 days** of final delivery.
- Paid upfront by card / PayPal link.
- **Pilot Guarantee [CLIENT COPY]:** *"If your first pilot video doesn't match the brief after two revision rounds, we'll refund the pilot in full."*
- **Capacity cap:** max **4 pilots in production at once** (~5 editing hours each).

### Why $149 and not $99 or $199 (decision rationale)
- **$99** signals "cheap", attracts deal-hunters, and undervalues ~5 hours of editing.
- **$199** for 3 videos (~$66/video) costs more per video than the Momentum plan — that teaches the wrong price anchor.
- **$149** ≈ $50/video = exactly the Momentum per-video price, so the pilot previews real retainer economics. It's a low-friction card purchase for a business owner (no approval needed), high enough to filter tyre-kickers, and the 14-day credit removes risk *and* creates urgency to decide.

## 9. Scope Limits (Put These in the Agreement)

- One brand / one primary account per plan. Second brand = second plan (10% multi-brand discount).
- Max source footage per month as in §2. Extra source footage: $40 per additional hour reviewed.
- Unused videos roll over **one month only**, max 25% of the plan's monthly count, and only if the delay was not ours to fix (e.g., footage arrived late).
- Late footage shifts delivery dates; it does not create rush obligations.
- **A revision** = changes within the original brief (trim, text fix, swap B-roll, change music, adjust captions). **Not a revision** = a new concept, new footage, or a changed brief → counts as a new video.
- Revision requests must be consolidated (one message per round) within **5 business days** of delivery; after that the video is considered approved.
- Not included: filming or on-location shoots, paid-ads management or media buying, influencer sourcing, custom 3D/character animation, voice-over talent, music beyond our licensed library, translation into languages we can't quality-check.
- Stock footage from our included libraries only; premium stock billed at cost with approval.
- Music: from our licensed library, or the client adds platform-native audio when posting.
- The client is responsible for the accuracy and legality of claims in their content (health, finance, legal, earnings) and for any third-party rights in footage they supply.
