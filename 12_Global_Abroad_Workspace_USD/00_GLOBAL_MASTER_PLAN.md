## 1. The Bet

English-speaking business owners already pay for short-form video editing and account management, and the owner's market read is that ~$2,000/month for one account is a normal price abroad. We can deliver studio-quality work **overnight** for North America and Australia/NZ because of the time difference. We sell that speed plus a quality system at **$500 / $1,000 / $2,000 a month**, start small with a **$149 pilot**, and build proof fast through free samples instead of faking case studies.

**Funnel assumptions (planning guesses — replace with real data after 2–4 weeks):**
- ~800–1,000 first touches/month once email is warmed (Akarsh, ~3.5 hrs/day).
- ~3% positive replies → 24–30/month.
- ~45% of positive replies book a call → 11–14 calls/month.
- ~40% of calls buy a pilot or plan; ~50% of pilots convert → **1–3 new retainers/month**, ramping as referrals, Upwork reviews and portfolio grow.
- Blended retainer ≈ $800/month. Sales cycle 1–4 weeks.

**Rough unit economics (Momentum, per month):** $1,000 revenue − payment fees (~1–4%, verify) − outsourced editing (~$180 if a freelancer edits) − tools share (~$30) ≈ **$750–$780 contribution** before team pay and tax. Phase-1 tool budget estimate: **$150–$250/month** (verify).
