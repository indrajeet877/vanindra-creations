### Footage hand-off standards (send to every client)
- **Where:** a shared folder per client. Options: Google Drive (default), Dropbox, or Frame.io (best for review). Pick one per client and stick to it.
- **How to upload:** one subfolder per recording day: `YYYY-MM-DD_[topic]`.
- **Recording tips (one-pager to client):** phone camera vertical 9:16, 1080p or 4K at 30 fps; face a window or soft light; lapel / wireless mic; quiet room; 2 seconds of silence before and after each take; one topic per clip; restart the sentence if you stumble — don't stop recording.
- **Long-form sources:** podcast / webinar files at the highest quality available, plus a transcript if they have one.
- **Cut-off times for next-business-day delivery** (see §3 table): footage + brief must be complete by the cut-off.

## 5. QA Checklist (12 Points — Every Video, No Exceptions)

Vandana Yadav (or Akarsh Yadav as backup) ticks this before any video goes to a client.

1. **Hook:** the first 1–2 seconds show the strongest line or visual, plus on-screen hook text (≤ 7 words). The video pays off what the hook promises.
2. **Captions:** 100% accurate — names, brand terms, numbers; US or UK spelling per client; captions never cover the face.
3. **Safe zones:** text and key visuals clear of platform UI (bottom and right edge on Reels / TikTok / Shorts — check current overlays per platform).
4. **Pacing:** no unintended dead air; clean jump cuts; no glitches, black frames or flash frames.
5. **Audio:** dialogue clear and consistent (aim around −14 LUFS integrated, peaks ≤ −1 dBTP); music ducked under speech; no clipping, hum or echo.
6. **Visuals:** consistent colour and exposure; B-roll relevant and licensed; no stock or app watermarks.
7. **Brand kit:** correct fonts, colours, logo placement, intro / outro.
8. **Claims & compliance:** no health, financial, legal or earnings claims beyond the client's own words; anything risky flagged in the delivery note; no identifiable patients, minors or third parties without client-confirmed consent.
9. **CTA & ending:** correct CTA for this video (per plan); clean loop or end frame.
10. **Cover / thumbnail:** title readable at small size; checks the profile-grid crop (verify each platform's current crop).
11. **Export specs:** 9:16 at 1080×1920 (plus 4:5 at 1080×1350 where the plan includes it), MP4 / H.264, source frame rate, high bitrate; filename per convention.
12. **Delivery package:** post caption, keywords / hashtags, suggested posting day, SRT file if requested; uploaded to the right folder; review link limited to the client.

**Vandana's final gate — "what not to post":** Would we proudly show this in our own portfolio? Is anything off-brand, insensitive, misleading or risky for this client? If no / yes → back to edit.

**Filename convention:** `[CLIENT]_[YYYYMMDD]_[NN]_[short-topic]_v[version]_[9x16|4x5].mp4` → e.g., `ACMEPOD_20261110_03_sleep-myths_v2_9x16.mp4`

## 6. Revision Policy (Matches the Agreement)

- Included rounds: **Pilot 2 · Spark 1 · Momentum 2 · Full Studio 2 (+1 full redo per month).**
- A **revision** = changes within the original brief (trim, text fix, B-roll swap, music change, caption tweak).
- A **new video** = new concept, new footage or a changed brief → counts against the monthly total.
- The client sends **one consolidated set of comments per round** (time-stamped comments on the review link).
- Revisions are returned within **1 business day**.
- No comments within **5 business days** of delivery → the video is approved.
- Extra rounds beyond the plan: $20 per video per round (waive once per month as goodwill).

## 9. Adding Freelance Editors (Contractors)

**Trigger:** signed editing load reaches **40 hours/month** (start recruiting) → editor live **before** load reaches 50 hours or before the 3rd retainer / 1st Full Studio.

1. **Source** (Akarsh): referrals, university networks, LinkedIn, editor communities on Instagram, freelance platforms. Look for 9:16 talking-head portfolios with clean captions.
2. **Paid test:** ₹500–₹1,000 for one test video from non-client footage, using our brief + QA checklist, 48-hour deadline.
3. **Score (0–10 each):** hook, caption accuracy, pacing, audio, follows the brief, speed, communication. **Pass = average ≥ 8.**
4. **Contractor agreement** (have it reviewed — see Payments & Legal doc): confidentiality, **IP assignment to VanIndra Creations** (so we can transfer rights to clients), no direct contact with or solicitation of our clients, delete footage after each project, payment per approved video. Ask the CA about TDS / tax on contractor payments.
5. **Pay:** budget ₹500–₹900 per approved short depending on style (assumption — confirm through tests); pay weekly or fortnightly.
6. **Onboard:** client style guide + QA checklist + 3 reference edits. Start on **one** client.
7. **QA:** Akarsh reviews **100%** of their first 20 videos, then spot-checks 1 in 5 (Vandana's final QA continues on everything).
8. **Access:** only their assigned client folders; 2-factor authentication; access removed the day they stop.
9. **Bench:** always keep 1–2 tested backup editors for holidays, exams and spikes.

## 11. Holidays, Coverage & Escalation

- Send each client the **studio holiday calendar** at onboarding and every January (major Indian holidays and any planned leave, including exam periods).
- Before a holiday: Full Studio posts are pre-scheduled; deliveries shift by one business day and are confirmed in writing a week ahead.
- **Escalation path:** client issue → Smita Yadav (same day) → Vandana Yadav (creative / final decision) → resolution or a written plan within 24 hours.
- **Missed deadline caused by us:** apply the Delivery Guarantee (video credited), apologise once, explain the fix, don't over-explain.

---

## 12. Data, Security & Retention

- 2-factor authentication on every studio account (email, cloud storage, social, scheduling).
- Per-client folders; freelancers see only what they work on.
- **Raw footage:** deleted 60 days after the end of the month it was delivered in, unless the client asks us to keep it.
- **Finals:** kept 12 months, then archived or deleted on request.
- Never post client work in our portfolio without **written** permission.
- Clinics / health clients: no footage of identifiable patients unless the client confirms written patient consent. We don't take on work that requires a US HIPAA business-associate agreement until a lawyer confirms we can.

## 13. Offboarding

1. Final batch delivered; confirm nothing is outstanding.
2. Share a final folder with all finals and project-free exports.
3. Remove all access we hold (and ask the client to confirm).
4. Short exit survey (3 questions: what worked, what didn't, would you recommend us?).
5. If they were happy: ask for a testimonial / referral. If not: log the lesson in the Friday review.
6. Delete raw footage per §12.
