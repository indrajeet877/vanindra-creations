## 4. Tone Guide — Native US / UK Business English

### The voice in five words
**Clear. Warm. Specific. Confident. Brief.**

### Rules
1. **Short sentences.** One idea per sentence. Most emails under 120 words; cold emails under 90.
2. **Contractions are good.** "We'll", "you're", "it's" sound human. "We will be delighted to assist you" sounds like a bank.
3. **Specific beats superlative.** "Next-day delivery, 20 videos a month" beats "world-class lightning-fast premium service".
4. **Lead with them, not us.** First line is about their show, clinic, product or post — never "We are a leading agency…".
5. **One question or one ask per message.**
6. **No hype, no ALL CAPS, max one exclamation mark per email** (zero is better in cold email).
7. **Emojis:** none in email or proposals; at most one in a DM.
8. **Spelling:** US clients → US spelling (color, optimize, program). UK / IE / AU / NZ clients → UK spelling (colour, optimise, programme). The website uses US spelling.
9. **Dates and times:** "Tuesday, Nov 10" or "10 November" — never "10/11" (US and UK read it differently). Always state the client's time zone: "9:30 am ET". Say "India time", never "IST" (Ireland uses "IST" for Irish Standard Time).
10. **Money:** "$500/month (USD)". Never mention ₹, lakh or crore in client copy.
11. **Greetings:** "Hi Sarah," — never "Dear Sir/Madam", "Respected", "Hello Sir".
12. **Sign-offs:** "Best," / "Thanks," (US) · "Cheers," / "Kind regards," (UK, AU, NZ, IE).

### Indian-English phrases to avoid in client copy → native replacement

| Avoid | Use instead |
| :--- | :--- |
| Kindly revert / revert back | Let me know / Reply when you can |
| Do the needful | Could you please [specific action]? |
| PFA / Please find attached | I've attached… / Here's… |
| Greetings of the day! | Hi [Name], |
| Hope you are doing good | Hope your week's going well (or skip it) |
| I have a doubt | I have a question |
| Prepone | Move earlier / bring forward |
| Today itself / now itself | Today / right away |
| Same to same | Exactly the same |
| Out of station | Travelling / out of office |
| Intimate you | Let you know |
| Discuss about | Discuss |
| As per our discussion | As we discussed / Following our call |
| Updation | Update |
| Passed out from (college) | Graduated from |
| Cent percent | 100% / completely |
| Sir / Ma'am (in writing) | First name |
| "Cheap", "low cost", "budget rates" | "Fair price", "a fraction of a local agency retainer" |
| "Hi dear" | "Hi [Name]" |
| "Waiting for your positive reply" | "Happy to send more details if helpful." |

## 5. What NOT to Say (Hard Rules)

1. **Never guarantee views, followers, leads, sales, virality or "going viral".** We guarantee deliverables, deadlines and quality — nothing the algorithm controls.
2. **Never invent** clients, logos, testimonials, case-study numbers or "as featured in". Use placeholders like `[client result]` until real.
3. **Never claim to be US / UK / AU based**, or use a fake Western name. We're proud of being an India-based studio — it's *why* we're fast.
4. **Never say "cheap", "Indian rates", "low cost labour"** or apologise for being overseas.
5. **Never name anyone other than Vandana Yadav, Smita Yadav and Akarsh Yadav** as part of the team. No nicknames, ever, in client channels.
6. **Never show any WhatsApp number other than `+91 81128 59437` (Smita Yadav).**
7. **Never ask for passwords.** Never offer to buy followers, engagement, reviews or "growth hacks".
8. **Never make health, financial or legal claims** on a client's behalf. Flag them; the client approves all claims.
9. **Never use copyrighted music** in client deliverables without a licence that covers commercial use.
10. **No India-market references** in global copy: no "1 lakh+ creators", no Diwali / wedding-season framing, no Hinglish, no "WhatsApp family group" jokes.
11. **Don't over-explain AI.** "We use AI tools for speed; every video is edited and checked by people" — that's the whole message.
12. **Don't disparage** freelancers, AI tools or other agencies by name.
