# Getting Paid in USD — Payments, Legal & Ops Checklist (India-Based Studio)

> **Important:** This is an operating checklist, **not tax or legal advice.** Payment-provider fees, availability and Indian tax/forex rules change often. Every item marked **(verify)** or **CONFIRM WITH CA** must be checked with the provider's current pricing page and with a Chartered Accountant (CA) before we invoice the first global client.
> **Owner:** Smita Yadav (billing & collections), with the owner and the CA for setup decisions.

## 1. Ways to Receive USD — Options Compared

| Option | How the client pays | Typical considerations (verify all) | Good for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Export-receivables platforms built for Indian service exporters** (e.g., Skydo, Xflow, Briskpe — and Payoneer, which is widely used by Indian freelancers) | Client sends a **local bank transfer** (ACH in the US, local transfer in UK/EU/AU) to account details the platform gives us; funds are converted and paid out to our Indian bank account | Fees: flat fee or % + FX margin — **compare the all-in INR received on a $1,000 test**. Payout: typically 1–3 business days (verify). KYC: business documents, PAN, bank account, sometimes GST. **Ask each one whether they issue FIRA / e-FIRA / FIRC for every payment** and whether they are authorised for cross-border payments (verify). | **Retainers** — feels like a domestic transfer to a US/UK/AU client; low chargeback risk |
| **Razorpay (international payments)** | Card payment link / invoice; Razorpay also markets an export account product for receiving foreign transfers (verify) | Needs international payments activated on the account (documentation + approval). Card fees and FX charges higher than bank transfer (verify). Card chargebacks possible. | **Pilots ($149) and add-ons** — quick card checkout |
| **PayPal (India)** | PayPal balance or card | Clients everywhere know it. Fees for international commercial payments + currency conversion tend to be on the higher side (verify current rates). Indian accounts generally pay out automatically to the linked bank account (verify). Buyer-protection disputes / chargebacks possible. Ask about FIRC / purpose-code handling. | Backup option; clients who insist on PayPal |
| **Stripe** | Cards, Stripe invoices | Onboarding of new Indian businesses has been restricted / invite-only (verify current status). If available: excellent invoicing and checkout. Card chargebacks possible. | Only if we can get an account |
| **Wise Business** | Client pays from their Wise or bank | Check whether an **India-registered** business can open an account and receive USD account details — availability has been limited (verify). Even if we can't hold an account, many clients can **pay** via Wise to our Indian bank. | Client-side convenience |
| **Direct SWIFT wire to our Indian current account** | Client's bank sends an international wire | Simple paper trail — our bank issues the inward-remittance documentation. But: client's outgoing wire fee, intermediary/correspondent bank deductions, and the bank's FX spread; 1–5 business days (verify). | Large or prepaid amounts (quarterly / annual) |
| **Platform payouts** (Upwork, Contra, Fiverr) | Client pays the platform | Platform fee first, then the withdrawal method's fee/FX (verify). Keep these clients on-platform per platform terms. | Clients won through those platforms |

**Not accepted:** crypto, gift cards, payments to any personal account, payments from a third party who isn't the client (unless the CA approves).

### Suggested set-up (confirm with CA before choosing)
1. **Primary (retainers):** one export-receivables platform that gives us local USD (and ideally GBP/AUD) account details and per-payment FIRA / e-FIRA.
2. **Secondary (pilots, add-ons, card-only clients):** a card payment link (Razorpay international or PayPal).
3. **Large prepayments:** direct wire to the business current account.
4. **Run a $1 to $10 test** on each rail before the first real invoice; write down the exact INR received and documents produced.

## 2. Chargeback & Payment-Risk Controls

Card and PayPal payments can be disputed by the payer, sometimes months later (windows vary — verify). Bank transfers are much harder to reverse.

- [ ] **Payment in advance** — every month, every pilot. No work starts before payment clears.
- [ ] **Signed agreement** before any retainer (e-sign audit trail kept).
- [ ] **Written approvals:** keep review-link comments and approval emails (deemed approval after 5 business days is in the agreement).
- [ ] **Delivery log:** date/time of every delivery (the review link history + delivery emails).
- [ ] Payment descriptor / invoice name matches **"VanIndra Creations"** so clients recognise the charge.
- [ ] Clear refund policy linked on invoices and the website.
- [ ] Late payment → reminder day 3 → work paused day 5 (per agreement).
- [ ] For prepaid quarters / years, prefer bank transfer over card.

## 3. CONFIRM WITH A CHARTERED ACCOUNTANT (Book This in Week 1)

Bring this list to the first meeting. Write the CA's answers next to each item and save them in this folder as `02_CA_Answers_[date].md`.

### Business set-up
- [ ] Which **legal structure** should invoice global clients (sole proprietorship, LLP, private limited)? In whose name and PAN? Any change needed as revenue grows?
- [ ] **Business current account** — which bank, and does it handle inward foreign remittances smoothly?
- [ ] Is **Udyam (MSME) registration** useful for us?
- [ ] Any **state registrations** needed (e.g., shop & establishment, professional tax)?
- [ ] Is an **IEC (Importer-Exporter Code)** needed for exporting services in our case, or only for specific benefits?

### GST
- [ ] Do we need **GST registration** given our expected turnover and export-only clients? Is it beneficial anyway (e.g., to claim input tax credit on software subscriptions)?
- [ ] Do our services qualify as **"export of services" / zero-rated supply**? (Conditions to check: supplier in India, recipient outside India, place of supply outside India, payment received in convertible foreign exchange, not supplied between establishments of the same person.)
- [ ] File a **Letter of Undertaking (LUT)** to export without paying IGST — how, when, and the annual renewal.
- [ ] Correct **SAC code(s)** for our services (video editing / audiovisual post-production vs. advertising / social-media services vs. content writing) — one code or several?
- [ ] Exact **invoice wording and fields** required for export invoices, including the LUT statement.
- [ ] Which **exchange rate** to use for GST reporting, and how to report exports in returns.
- [ ] Treatment of **platform income** (Upwork / Contra / Fiverr) and platform fees.

### Foreign exchange (FEMA / RBI) & bank paperwork
- [ ] Documents needed per payment: **FIRC / FIRA / e-FIRA / e-BRC** — which ones, and from whom (bank vs. payment platform)?
- [ ] Correct **purpose code** for our inward remittances, and how to make sure the bank/platform tags it correctly.
- [ ] The **time limit for realising export proceeds** and what to do if a client pays late.
- [ ] Whether each payment platform we're considering is acceptable for compliance purposes.

### Income tax
- [ ] Which tax regime and whether any **presumptive taxation scheme** applies to our activity.
- [ ] **Advance tax** schedule.
- [ ] Deductible expenses: software, freelancers, equipment, internet, coworking, platform fees.
- [ ] **TDS / tax obligations on payments to Indian freelance editors.**
- [ ] US clients asking for **Form W-8BEN / W-8BEN-E**: which form applies to us and how to fill it (including any India–US tax-treaty claim).
- [ ] Other countries' clients asking for tax forms (UK, AU, CA) — what to provide.

### Bookkeeping
- [ ] Monthly reconciliation format: USD invoiced ↔ USD received ↔ fees ↔ INR credited ↔ FIRA/FIRC on file.
- [ ] Accounting software recommendation (e.g., Zoho Books, Tally, or the CA's preference).

### Legal (lawyer, not CA — or the CA's referral)
- [ ] Review the **service agreement** (§4) and **contractor agreement** once; decide governing law and dispute resolution.
- [ ] Are e-signed agreements with foreign clients enforceable as we've drafted them? Any stamp-duty considerations?
- [ ] **Privacy:** India's DPDP Act obligations, plus GDPR / UK GDPR for clients' and leads' personal data; privacy policy for the website.
- [ ] **Cold-email compliance** by country (CAN-SPAM, CASL, UK PECR, AU Spam Act) — see ICP doc §4.
- [ ] Health clients: patient-consent handling; confirm we should **not** take work requiring a US HIPAA business-associate agreement.
- [ ] Trademark search / registration for "VanIndra Creations" (India first; abroad later if needed).

## 4. Service Agreement — Outline (Have a Lawyer Review Once)

1. **Parties & effective date** — VanIndra Creations (legal entity name, address) and the client.
2. **Services** — the plan chosen; deliverables in **Schedule A** (copied from the Offer Ladder).
3. **Term & cancellation** — month-to-month; renews automatically each billing date; either party may cancel with **15 days' notice** before the next billing date.
4. **Fees & payment** — USD, **monthly in advance**, due on receipt; payment methods; late payment: reminder at day 3, work paused at day 5; prepay discounts; founding-client rate (if any) and its end date; each party responsible for its own taxes.
5. **Client responsibilities** — supply footage, brand kit and access on time; hold the rights to all supplied material and consents from people who appear in it; accuracy and legality of all claims (health, financial, legal, earnings); timely approvals.
6. **Turnaround, revisions & approvals** — business-day definition (India time, holiday calendar in **Schedule C**); revision rounds and what counts as a revision vs. a new video (**Schedule B**); deemed approval after 5 business days.
7. **Intellectual property** — client owns final deliverables on full payment; studio keeps its pre-existing templates, presets and know-how; project files not included unless purchased; portfolio use **only with the client's written permission**.
8. **Licensed materials** — music and stock are licensed for use inside the delivered videos only; client must not extract them for other uses.
9. **Confidentiality** — mutual; survives termination.
10. **Data & security** — access via delegated permissions (no passwords); retention (raw footage deleted 60 days after delivery month unless asked otherwise); breach notification.
11. **Subcontractors** — studio may use vetted contractors bound by confidentiality and IP assignment; studio remains responsible for their work.
12. **Guarantees** — Delivery Guarantee and Pilot Guarantee (exact wording from the Offer Ladder §8).
13. **No performance guarantee** — no promise of views, followers, engagement, leads or revenue; platforms control distribution and may change rules.
14. **Limitation of liability** — capped at fees paid in the previous [1–3] months (lawyer to set); no indirect or consequential damages.
15. **Indemnities** — client for supplied materials and claims; studio for materials the studio added.
16. **Termination for cause** — material breach not fixed within 10 days; on any termination, client pays for work delivered and receives finished work.
17. **Non-solicitation** — neither party hires the other's staff or contractors for 12 months (agency / white-label version adds end-client non-solicitation).
18. **Force majeure** — including power, internet and platform outages beyond reasonable control.
19. **Independent contractor** relationship.
20. **Governing law & disputes** — to be decided with the lawyer (e.g., Indian law with online arbitration/mediation, or another approach for larger clients).
21. **General** — entire agreement, amendments in writing, e-signatures valid, notices by email.

**Schedules:** A — Plan & deliverables · B — Revision & approval policy · C — Studio holiday calendar.

**E-signature tools (pick one, verify pricing):** Zoho Sign, Dropbox Sign, DocuSign, PandaDoc.

## 5. Invoice Template — Fields

> Final heading and wording **CONFIRM WITH CA** (export invoices under LUT have specific requirements).

| Field | Example / note |
| :--- | :--- |
| Document heading | "Invoice" / "Tax Invoice" — per CA |
| Invoice number | Sequential per financial year, e.g., `VC/EXP/26-27/001` (format per CA) |
| Invoice date · Due date | Due on receipt (billed in advance) |
| Supplier | Legal name, trade name **VanIndra Creations**, registered address, GSTIN (if registered), PAN (if CA advises), email, `+91 81128 59437` |
| LUT reference | LUT ARN + date, and the export statement wording from the CA |
| Client (bill to) | Legal / business name, contact person, full billing address, **country**, client tax ID if they provide one |
| Place of supply | Client's country (outside India) |
| Line items | Description (e.g., "Momentum plan — 20 short-form videos — 1–30 Nov 2026"), SAC code (per CA), quantity, unit price (USD), amount (USD) |
| Adjustments | Pilot credit (−$149), founding-client rate, prepay discount, Delivery Guarantee credits |
| Tax line | Per CA (e.g., IGST at 0% under LUT, if that's the advice) |
| Totals | Subtotal, total **in USD**, amount in words |
| Payment instructions | Payment link and/or account details from the chosen rail; "Use invoice number as reference" |
| Terms | "Work for this period begins once payment is received." · Link to Terms & Refund Policy |
| Authorised signatory | Name + digital signature |

**Internal record per invoice (not on the invoice):** USD received, fees deducted, INR credited, exchange rate, date credited, FIRA/FIRC/e-FIRA file name, purpose code used.

**Invoicing tools (options — verify pricing and export-invoice support):** Zoho Invoice / Zoho Books, Razorpay invoices, the receivables platform's own invoicing, or a Google Docs template until volume grows.
