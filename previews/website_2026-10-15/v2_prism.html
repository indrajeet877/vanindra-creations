08FAQ

      

        
Questions people ask us first.

      

    

    

      

        
Something else? Ask our WhatsApp desk.

        
      

      

        

          I don't know editing or scriptwriting. Can I still work with you?
          
Yes. You record raw talking videos on your phone — about 30–60 minutes a week — and send them on WhatsApp. We handle the scripts, 3-second hooks, editing, bilingual captions, 9:16 covers and brand positioning.

        

        

          Can I see a sample edit of my own video before choosing a plan?
          
Yes. Message our official WhatsApp desk (Smita Yadav · +91 81128 59437), or use the message box at the end of this page. We'll share a free 7-day content audit and a 15-second before/after sample edit of one of your videos before you commit.

        

        

          Do you sell followers, or guarantee views and deals?
          
No to both. We never buy followers, bots or engagement — they damage reach — and no honest agency can guarantee views or deals. We commit to deliverables and deadlines: every Reel is checked against our 4 retention rules and arrives on time.

        

        

          Why not just use ChatGPT or a ₹500 freelance editor?
          
Cheap freelance editors rarely know which 3-second hook holds a 1 lakh+ audience, or what brands look for before sponsoring a creator. And prompting AI tools yourself still eats hours of your day.

        

        

          How will I track my scripts, Reels and monthly growth?
          
You get a private WhatsApp group with founder Vandana Yadav, Smita Yadav and Akarsh Yadav, plus a shared tracker for every script, Reel draft and monthly report. Our client portal is in preview.
