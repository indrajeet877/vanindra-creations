## 2. The 3 Rules of Tagging & Attribution

Whenever a Reel goes live on `@vandanay2012`, `@smitayadav_0901`, or `@yadavakarsh_07`:

### Rule A: Invite Collaborator (On 50% of Reels)
- Before hitting Share on Instagram -> Tap **Tag People** -> **Invite Collaborator** -> Select `@[AgencyHandle]`.
- Accept the collaboration invite immediately from the Agency Instagram account.
- Now the agency page displays Reels with thousands of views right on its main grid!

### Rule B: Caption Attribution & Tag (On 100% of Reels)
