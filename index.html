Questions, answered plainly.

Where is your team based?

In India. That is how overnight delivery works for the United States, Canada, and Australia. You upload at the end of your day. Drafts are usually waiting when you start work. London usually has them by lunchtime the next day. The UAE is on the next business day, because our hours overlap more of your afternoon.

What do I need to send you?

Raw video from your phone or camera, or a long piece such as a podcast, webinar, or talk. We send a one-page recording guide on day one.

Do you write scripts?

On Momentum, up to 8 a month. On Full Studio, every original video. On Spark, we work from your footage and write the hook text and on-screen titles.

Which platforms do you edit for?

Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, and Facebook. Momentum and Full Studio include platform-specific versions.

How many revisions do I get?

One round per video on Spark. Two on Momentum, Full Studio, and the $149 trial. Changes come back the next business day.

How fast is delivery?

Next business day on Momentum and Full Studio. Two business days on Spark. Five business days for the trial. The clock starts when we have the complete footage.

Do you guarantee views or followers?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. We guarantee the number of videos, the deadlines, and the quality bar. We report on what happened and adjust the next batch. We never use bots or paid engagement.

Who owns the videos?

You do, once the invoice is paid.

Do you need my passwords?

Never. Posting uses partner access through Meta Business Suite, YouTube permissions, or your scheduling tool, so you can remove us in one click.

Is the music licensed?

Yes. Anything we add comes from a licensed library, or you add platform audio when you post.

Do you use AI?

Yes, for speed: transcription, a first pass at clip finding, and caption timing. A person edits every video, and a person checks it, before you see it.

How do payments work, and can I cancel?

Plans are billed monthly in advance, in USD, by card or bank transfer, with an invoice each month. Cancel any time with 15 days’ notice before the next billing date. There is no long-term contract. The $149 trial is a single payment.

Can I buy one project and stop?

Yes. Start with the $149 trial, or ask for a larger one-time batch on the call. If the first trial video does not match the brief after two revision rounds, we refund the trial in full.
