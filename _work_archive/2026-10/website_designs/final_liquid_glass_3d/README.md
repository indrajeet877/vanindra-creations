# Sources: final Liquid Glass 3D website (15 Oct 2026)

- `base_liquid_glass_fixed.html`: the team-pick Liquid Glass with every judge fix, before the effects were added.
- `homepage_src/`: the effects layer. `fx.css` and `fx.js` hold the cursor, motion and intro; `glass.js` is the WebGL glass ring; `build.py` merges them into the base. The other files are the QA and recording scripts.
- `EFFECTS_homepage.md`: every effect, where it is, how it degrades (phones, reduced motion, no WebGL, JS off), and the performance numbers.
- `NOTES_login_and_studio.md` and `verify_pages.js`: the redesign notes and the checker for `login.html` and `thumbnail-studio.html`.
- `MOTION_BRIEF.md`: the brief the effects were built to.

The finished pages are in `previews/website_2026-10-15_final/`.
