      FAQ
      
Questions creators & business owners ask us first.

      

        
Something else on your mind? Ask our official WhatsApp desk directly.

        
      

    

    

      

        I don't know video editing or scriptwriting at all — can I still work with you?
        
Absolutely. Our studio is built for busy creators, doctors, educators and business owners. You record raw talking videos on your phone — about 30–60 minutes a week — and send them on WhatsApp. We handle the scripts, 3-second hooks, editing, bilingual captions, 9:16 covers and brand positioning.

      

      

        Can I see a sample edit of my own video before choosing a plan?
        
Yes. Write to our official WhatsApp desk (Smita Yadav · +91 81128 59437), or use the message box at the top of this page, and send one recent video. You get a free page audit and a 15-second sample edit of that video, back on WhatsApp within 2 working days of receiving it — before you commit to anything.

      

      

        Do you sell followers, or guarantee views and deals?
        
No to both. We never buy followers, bots or engagement — they damage reach — and no honest agency can promise views or deals. What we do commit to is deliverables and deadlines: every Reel passes our 4 retention rules and arrives on time.

      

      

        Why not just use ChatGPT or a ₹500 freelance editor?
        
Cheap freelance editors rarely know which 3-second hook holds a 1 lakh+ audience, or what D2C brands look for before sponsoring a creator. And prompting AI tools yourself still eats hours of your day.

      

      

        How will I track my scripts, edited Reels and monthly growth?
        
You get a private WhatsApp group with founder Vandana Yadav, Smita Yadav and Akarsh Yadav, plus a shared tracker listing every script, Reel draft and monthly report. Our client portal is in preview.
