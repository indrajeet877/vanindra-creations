        05QuestionsFive things people ask first
        

          
    
            
  • I don't know editing or scriptwriting — can I still work with you?
    Yes. You record simple videos on your phone — about 30–60 minutes a week — and send them on WhatsApp. We handle scripts, hooks, editing, captions, 9:16 covers and brand positioning.
    • 
            
  • Can I see a sample edit of my own video before choosing a plan?
    Yes. Message our official WhatsApp desk (Smita Yadav · +91 81128 59437) from the message box at the end of this page. We'll send a free 7-day audit and a 15-second sample edit of one of your videos.
    • 
            
  • Do you sell followers, or guarantee views and deals?
    No to both. We never buy followers or bots, and no honest agency can guarantee views or deals. We commit to deliverables and deadlines.
    • 
            
  • Why not just use ChatGPT or a ₹500 freelance editor?
    Cheap editors rarely know which 3-second hook holds a 1 lakh+ audience, or what brands look for before sponsoring. And prompting AI tools yourself still takes hours.
    • 
            
  • How will I track my scripts, Reels and monthly growth?
    You get a private WhatsApp group with founder Vandana Yadav, Smita Yadav and Akarsh Yadav, plus a shared tracker listing every script, draft and monthly report.
