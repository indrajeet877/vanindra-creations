          FAQ
          
Questions creators & owners ask us first.

          
Something else on your mind? Ask Smita directly on WhatsApp.

          
        

        

          

            I don't know video editing or scriptwriting at all — can I still work with you?
            
Absolutely — the studio is built for busy creators, doctors, educators and business owners. You record raw talking videos on your smartphone and send them over WhatsApp. Our team handles your scripts, 3-second hooks, editing, bilingual captions, 9:16 covers and brand positioning.

          

          

            Can I see a sample edit of my own video before choosing a monthly plan?
            
Yes. Message our official WhatsApp desk (Smita Yadav · +91 81128 59437) using the composer at the top of the page. We provide a complimentary 7-day content audit and a 15-second before/after sample edit on one of your existing videos before you commit.

          

          

            Why not just use ChatGPT or a ₹500 freelance editor?
            
Cheap freelance editors don't know which 3-second hook holds a 1 lakh+ audience, or what D2C brands look for before sponsoring a creator. And prompting AI tools yourself still eats hours of your day. We combine AI speed with human accountability, led by founder Vandana Yadav.

          

          

            Do you sell fake followers or bots, or is this organic growth?
            
We never use fake followers or bots — they permanently damage reach. Every view, share, follower and brand enquiry is earned organically through strong 3-second hooks, high-retention editing and strategic Collab posts.

          

          

            Do you guarantee views or followers?
            
No — no honest studio can, because nobody controls the algorithm. We guarantee the work: researched hooks, scripts, studio edits and on-time delivery — and we review the results with you every month.

          

          

            How will I track my weekly scripts, edited Reels and monthly growth?
            
The moment you onboard, you get a dedicated WhatsApp studio group with founder Vandana Yadav, Smita Yadav and Akarsh Yadav, plus access to your client portal, where every script, Reel draft and monthly report is tracked.
