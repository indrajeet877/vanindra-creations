08FAQ

        
Questions people ask first

        
Still unsure? Message Smita Yadav directly on the official WhatsApp desk.

        
      

      

        

          I don't know editing or scriptwriting at all — can I still work with you?
          
Absolutely. The studio is built for busy creators, doctors, educators and business owners. You record raw talking videos on your phone and send them on WhatsApp; we handle scripts, 3-second hooks, editing, bilingual captions, covers and brand positioning.

        

        

          Can I see a sample edit of my own video before choosing a plan?
          
Yes. Message the official WhatsApp desk (Smita Yadav · +91 81128 59437) using the composer at the top. We send a free 7-day content audit and a 15-second before/after sample edit of one of your existing videos before you commit.

        

        

          Why not just use ChatGPT or a ₹500 freelance editor?
          
A cheap editor doesn't know which 3-second hook holds a 1 lakh+ audience, or what brands check before sponsoring a creator. And prompting AI yourself still eats hours every day. With us you get AI speed plus human accountability, led by founder Vandana Yadav.

        

        

          Do you guarantee views or followers?
          
No — no honest studio can. We commit to the work: scripts, edits, covers and reports delivered on schedule. Growth comes from consistent, high-retention content, and we show you every number in your portal.

        

        

          Do you use fake followers or bots?
          
Never. Bots permanently damage reach. Every view, share and enquiry comes organically from strong 3-second hooks, high-retention editing and strategic collab posts.

        

        

          How do I track scripts, edits and monthly growth?
          
You get a dedicated WhatsApp studio pod with founder Vandana Yadav, Smita Yadav and Akarsh Yadav, plus a client portal login where every script, Reel draft and monthly report is tracked.
