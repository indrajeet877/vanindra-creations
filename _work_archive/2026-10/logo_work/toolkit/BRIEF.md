## 3. What the owner wants
Elegant. Premium. "Best level" — the kind of identity a top studio (Pentagram, Koto, Wolff Olins, a Paris maison) would present. It must feel crafted and intentional, never cartoonish, never "blended", never generic. It has to work for Indian premium creators AND for a $2,000/month client in London or New York.

## 4. Why the earlier work failed (do not repeat)
- Current crest: glossy 3D gold, wings, bead, bevels, gradients → cheap, clip-art, cartoonish, unreadable small.
- Round 1: a stock font "V" dropped into a container (arch / circle / phone frame) + a stock font wordmark. No idea, no custom drawing, nothing ownable. Container-plus-letter is the most generic move in logo design.

## 5. Hard rules
1. **Custom construction is mandatory.** No unmodified font letter as the mark. Use real drawing: ligatures, interlocks with over/under breaks, shared stems, cuts, negative space, custom geometry built from a grid. Fonts may be a starting skeleton only.
2. **No letter-in-a-container** (no V in a circle/square/arch/frame/shield).
3. **Flat**: 1–2 flat colours. No gradients, 3D, bevels, shadows, glows, textures. Must also work in ONE colour (all emerald, all ivory, all gold).
4. **Avoid clichés & sensitive symbols**: lotus (political association in India), peacock/feather, Om, trishul, vajra/thunderbolt icon, deities, flags, map of India, crowns, wings, play-button triangle, camera/film-strip/clapper, generic swoosh, lightbulb, rocket.
5. **Small sizes**: distinct and legible at 16 px (favicon) and as a 110 px circular Instagram profile picture. Provide a dedicated small-size version if needed.
6. **Craft**: optical balance (not just mathematical), consistent stroke logic, clean curves (no wobbly hand-typed béziers), deliberate negative space, generous clear space, custom-spaced wordmark (optical kerning).
7. Palette: emerald `#0B2A20`, ivory `#F6F1E7`, antique gold `#A8843A` (on light), soft gold `#D2B26A` (on dark). White `#FFFFFF` allowed.

- `explore.png` — your early sheet of ≥6 thumbnail variations
- `rationale.md` — concept name; one-sentence public story; construction notes; small-size behaviour; risks.
All text in SVGs must be outlined paths (the toolkit does this). Keep files under ~150 KB each.

## 7. Process (non-negotiable)
1. Look at the rejected work first. Then explore ≥6 genuinely different variations inside your territory, render them as one sheet, LOOK at it (Read the PNG), and pick the best 1–2.
2. Refine through at least 3 loops of: render → look at the PNG → critique against sections 4–5 → revise. Be brutally self-critical; if it looks like something a template site would sell, start again.
3. Before finishing, look at sizes.png and context.png. If the mark dies at 16–32 px, fix it.
