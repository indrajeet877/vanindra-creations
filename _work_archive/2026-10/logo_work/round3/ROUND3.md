## Where we are
- The owner rejected the 3D crest and Round 1 ("the worst logo you have ever created ... think more deeper").
- Round 2: seven hand-drawn concepts. All three judges independently chose **T5 Shirorekha** (overall 6.5 / 7 / 7). No concept was rated ready for a top client. The judges' shared criticism:
  - Six of seven were "a decorated V". Stop defaulting to a lone V.
  - Every lockup reused Round 1's tracked gold Jost "CREATIONS" descriptor. **Banned in Round 3.** If "Creations" appears, it must be drawn in the same system as the name, or omitted from the primary lockup.
  - Every horizontal lockup stuttered ("V VANINDRA", "VI VanIndra"). **Banned**: a horizontal lockup must never repeat the initial.
  - Ideas that only show up in a construction diagram don't count. The idea must be visible in the drawing.
  - "VI" as initials reads as the Roman numeral six and as India's "Vi" (Vodafone Idea) telecom brand. Avoid a standalone VI.
  - Full-width headline bars over Latin capitals can drift toward the "Samarkan / Indian-restaurant menu" font. Austere only: no curls, ornaments, foil or decoration.
  - Devanagari must be drawn in the same system as the Latin (not Tiro Devanagari pasted next to it) and is always marked "to be verified by the team". The conjunct द्र must be correct. वनिंद्रा is the spelling.
  - Pan-India and global: Devanagari can't be mandatory in every lockup (South Indian and foreign clients). Provide a clean global version for the USD workspace.
