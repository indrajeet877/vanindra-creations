# 02 — Capacity & Pricing Sanity Check

> **Question answered:** How many clients can Vandana Yadav, Smita Yadav and Akarsh Yadav serve *without missing deadlines*, and is any package priced below the work it promises?
> **Method:** time per Reel × Reels per package + fixed monthly work per client, compared with conservative available hours. All figures are **estimates** — replace them with real tracked time after the first 4 weeks (see §8).
> **Short answer:** today the team can safely deliver **about 24 client Reels a month** — that is **2 Starter clients, or 1 Pro Growth + 1 Trial** — plus a capped number of free samples. **360° VIP cannot be staffed at all** without a trained freelance editor and new SOPs. Editing (Akarsh Yadav) is the bottleneck.

## 5. When and How to Add Freelance Editors

### 5.1 Triggers — start onboarding when ANY is true
1. The Capacity Board shows **> 45 h editing booked** for any of the next 4 weeks (75% of 60 h).
2. We are about to sign a **3rd monthly client**, a **Pro while a Starter is active**, or **any VIP**.
3. **Two first-cut deadlines missed** in one month.
4. Akarsh's global lead-gen workload increases.

Recruiting + testing takes 2–3 weeks, so **start now** and keep **2 tested editors on the bench** before the second monthly client signs.

### 5.2 Process
1. **Source:** referrals, college networks (Prayagraj, Gorakhpur, Delhi), editor communities on Instagram/LinkedIn, freelance/internship portals.
2. **Paid test:** ₹300–₹500 for one 30–40 s Reel from in-house sample footage (with the on-camera person's permission), 24-hour deadline, scored on the 4 retention rules, Hindi/Hinglish caption accuracy, taste, deadline and communication.
3. **Agreement** (`04` L6): NDA, footage used only for the job, edits' IP assigned on payment, no portfolio use without written OK, **no direct client contact**, per-Reel rate, payment within 7 days of approval.
4. **Access:** only the client's `04_Raw_Footage_Upload` (view) and `05_Edits_for_Review` (upload). Never the official WhatsApp. Remove access when the job ends.
5. **QA:** Akarsh reviews every freelancer Reel — 30 min each for the first 10, then ~15 min.
6. **Client-facing:** freelancers are never named to clients; all communication stays with the official desk and the three public team members.

### 5.3 Cost assumption
- **₹600–₹1,000 per Reel** (30–45 s, incl. cover and 2 micro-revisions); planning figure **₹800**. Verify with 3 quotes and the paid test.
- Internal benchmark: `02_Market_Research_and_ICP/03_Indian_Competitor_Analysis_and_Winning_Edge.md` lists boutique editing agencies at ₹800–₹3,500 per Reel (editing only).
- A part-time freelancer ≈ 30 Reels/month.

## 7. Capacity Rules for Sales (put these on the Capacity Board)

1. Before quoting a start date, add the new client's editing hours (Reels × 2.5) to the board. **If any week goes above 75% (45 h/month equivalent), offer a later start or the waitlist.**
2. **No VIP sales** until Stage 1 is complete and the VIP SOPs (publishing access, DM tool, representation agreement) have run successfully on an in-house account for 2 weeks.
3. Stagger client shoot days so batches don't land on the same day.
4. In-house brand Reels and UGC packs go on the same board.
5. If a deadline is at risk, tell the client **before** it passes (T-23 in `01`).
