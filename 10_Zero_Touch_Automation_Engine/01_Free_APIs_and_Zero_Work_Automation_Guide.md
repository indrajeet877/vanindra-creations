## 1. The 4 Free Background APIs Running Inside `index.html`

| Free API Used | Cost | What It Automates in the Background | How It Saves Team Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Pollinations Free LLM API (`https://text.pollinations.ai/`)** | **₹0 (100% Free, No API Key Required)** | As soon as a creator submits the form on our website, the browser calls `text.pollinations.ai` in the background and writes their **Custom "What to Post" Rules + 3 Word-for-Word Viral Reel Scripts + Editing Cues for Sahil** in 5 seconds! | **Saves 30 mins per lead:** Indrajeet & Guggy don't have to manually write the initial audit—the client sends the *already-generated* AI plan to Guggy/Sahil on WhatsApp! |
| **2. Web Speech Recognition API (`webkitSpeechRecognition`)** | **₹0 (Built into Chrome/Android)** | Lets non-literate, semi-literate, or busy creators tap **🎤 "यहाँ दबाकर बोलें"** and speak in **Hindi, Hinglish, or English**. It automatically transcribes their voice into the requirement form! | Creators who struggle with typing or English can onboard in 20 seconds by simply talking into their phone! |
| **3. Web Speech Synthesis API (`window.speechSynthesis`)** | **₹0 (Built into Mobile/Desktop)** | Powers the **🔊 "हिंदी में सुनें (Listen in Hindi)"** buttons for the website overview, **Vandana Yadav's Flagship Client Testimonial**, and the **AI-Generated Plan**! | Even a creator who doesn't want to read long text can *listen* to our pitch and their custom plan in natural Hindi! |
| **4. WhatsApp Pre-Formatted Deep-Link + LocalStorage Auto-CRM** | **₹0 (100% Free)** | Bundles the creator's contact info + voice transcript + AI-generated scripts into a 1-tap WhatsApp payload directly to **Guggy (`+91 8112859437`)** or **Sahil (`+91 9415259408`)**. | Zero copy-pasting between forms and chats—everything arrives structured on WhatsApp! |
