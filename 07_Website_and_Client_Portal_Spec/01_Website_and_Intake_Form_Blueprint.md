## 1. What the Website Will Do (3 Core Functions)

### Function A: High-Converting Landing Page for 100K+ Influencers
- **Hero Headline:** *"You Built a 1 Lakh+ Audience. Let Us Handle Your Scripts, Editing & Growth — In Just 2 Hours of Shooting a Week."*
- **Live Showcase Grid:** Embedded/featured transformations from `@vandanay2012`, `@smitayadav_0901`, `@yadavakarsh_07`, and client Reels.
- **"Before vs. After" Interactive Slider:** Visitors can compare a raw phone clip vs. Sahil's high-retention edited Reel.
- **Clear Service Packages:** Starter (12 Reels), Pro (20 Reels), VIP (30 Reels), plus the **3-Reel Trial Pack**.

### Function B: Instant "Free 3-Hook Sample Generator" (Lead Magnet)
- When Guggy or Sahil sends an influencer to the website, the influencer can enter:
  - Their Instagram `@handle`
  - Their Follower Count (`100K–250K`, `250K–500K`, `500K+`)
  - Their Niche & Language (`Hindi`, `Hinglish`, `English`)
  - Their Biggest Struggle (`What to Post`, `High-Retention Editing`, `Hooks & Scripting`, `All of the Above`)
- This immediately captures their WhatsApp/Email for Guggy & Sahil and triggers their **Free Custom 7-Day Content Roadmap**!

### Function C: Full Client Requirement Intake Portal (Onboarding Form)
Once a client agrees to work with us, they fill out the **Creator Requirement Portal** on the website, which asks:
1. **Basic Info:** Name, `@handle`, Follower count, City, Preferred language (Hindi/Hinglish/English).
2. **Niche & Audience:** Who follows you? What are your top 3 best-performing Reels ever (paste links)?
3. **Boundaries ("What NOT to Post"):** Any topics, words, or styles you never want on your page?
4. **Monetization Goal:** Do you want more Brand Deals, Coaching/Course Sales, Clinic/Local Clients, or Pure Follower Growth?
5. **Visual Vibe Selection:** Pick from 3 editing styles (Fast Hormozi/Retention Style, Clean Aesthetic Lifestyle, Storyteller Documentary Style).
6. **Raw Footage Upload Link:** Direct button to upload their raw clips or link their Google Drive.

*Bonus Automation:* When the influencer submits this form on the website, it formats their answers into the **exact JSON/Markdown block needed for Indrajeet's `02_Master_LLM_Prompt_Pack.md`** so you can generate their entire monthly plan in 60 seconds!
