# Logo Round 3: the 2 finalists (15 Oct 2026)

> **Status:** for the team vote. Not approved; do not use on any public page yet.
> **Quick look:** open `F1_Shirorekha/board.jpg` and `F2_Hinge/board.jpg`. Each board shows the logo as an Instagram picture, website header, phone header, browser icon, Reel "Edit by" credit and one-colour versions.

## How we got here
- **Round 1** (3 simple options) and the old 3D gold crest were rejected.
- **Round 2:** 50 AI-image options plus 7 hand-built geometry logos (M1–M7). All 3 judges picked M5 **Shirorekha**.
- **Round 3:** M5 rebuilt with every judge fix, plus 3 new ideas (One Dot, The Hinge, Kolam Line), judged against M5. The best 2 then had two polish rounds and a final re-check by 3 judges:
  1. **Creative director:** taste and craft.
  2. **Indian client:** a 4-lakh creator and a jewellery/saree/skincare brand manager.
  3. **Global client:** a US client paying $2,000/month, plus a print and favicon expert.

| | Finalist | The idea | Final judges (out of 10) | Ready for team vote |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **F1** | **Shirorekha** | Every Hindi word hangs from one line (the *shirorekha*). VANINDRA hangs from that line too, and the symbol is the letter **व**. The "headline" is also the first 3 seconds of a Reel, which is what we sell. | **7.8 / 7.8 / 7.8** | Yes, recommended by all 3 judges |
| F2 | The Hinge | In "VanIndra" the last stroke of *Van* (forest) is the first stroke of *Indra* (sky), drawn in gold. The logo is "Van / Indra" on two lines, and the symbol is an "n" with a long gold leg. | 6.8 / 6.8 / 6.8 | Yes |

**Why the judges recommend F1:**
- It is the more original and more Indian system.
- It keeps the name as one word.
- Its gold व is the strongest small mark of all three rounds, from the 16 px browser icon to the 110 px Instagram picture.

**F2's main risks:**
- Its symbol reads as an "n" or a Greek η, not as VanIndra.
- On a Reel, "Van / Indra" can read like a person's name.
- In the US, "Van" alone reads as the vehicle.

## Decisions the team must make before F1 can go live
1. **Hindi check:** a native Hindi reader must approve the drawn वनिंद्रा, especially the द्र, the ि hook and the dot, and confirm that the symbol reads as **व** (not प or ब). Until then the Hindi files stay unused.
2. **One Instagram picture per account:** @vanindracreations can show only one picture. The judges suggest the gold **व** for the India account. The "VANINDRA on a gold line" picture is only for a separate global (USD) account, if we open one.
3. Small craft notes remain (some joins under CREATIONS). They will be cleaned up in a vector editor once the team picks a winner.
