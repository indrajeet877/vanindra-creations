## Why the current crest is being replaced
The current V-wing crest (`vanindra_creations_signature_crest.jpg`, used in the website header) relies on 3D gold wings, bevels, a glossy bead and gradients. It reads as clip-art rather than a premium studio, blurs at small sizes, and can't be reproduced in one colour.

## Files
Every direction comes in four forms, each on ivory and on emerald:
- `*_horizontal_on_*.svg`: primary lockup for website headers, letterheads and decks
- `*_stacked_on_*.svg`: centred lockup for covers, the end screen and social posts
- `*_icon_on_*.svg`: emblem for large use
- `*_icon_small_on_*.svg`: heavier version tuned for 16–64 px (favicon, Instagram profile picture, Reel watermark)
- `B_editorial_icon_filled_on_*.svg`: solid seal variant

All text is converted to outlines, so the files render identically everywhere with no fonts installed. Typefaces used: Bodoni Moda, Cormorant Garamond and Jost (all under the SIL Open Font License, free for commercial logo use).
