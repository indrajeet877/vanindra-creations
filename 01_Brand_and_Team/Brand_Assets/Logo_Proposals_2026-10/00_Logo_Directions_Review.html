Logo Directions

Three new directions to replace the current crest. Each one is drawn as clean vector outlines, works in a single colour, and stays readable from a billboard down to a browser tab.

Awaiting approval Nothing changes on the live website until the team picks one.

Why the crest is being replaced

  • The 3D gold wings, bevels and glossy bead read as clip-art, not as a premium studio.
  • Gradients and shadows turn into a blur at profile-picture and favicon sizes.
  • It cannot be printed or embossed in one colour, so it breaks on invoices, watermarks and merchandise.
Flat and preciseNo gradients, bevels or shadows.
One colour worksEmerald, ivory or gold alone.
Readable at 16 pxA dedicated small-size version for each.
India and abroadReads as premium in Delhi, London or New York.
A

Maison

A Bodoni capital V inside a slim arch. The arch is a 9:16 Reel frame and the doorway of an Indian palace; the gold diamond above the V completes "Vi". The wordmark is set in Bodoni, with CREATIONS spaced out between two gold rules.

Why it works

  • Has a story the team can tell in one sentence: the frame we create in, and the doorway clients walk through.
  • Bodoni is the typeface of fashion and luxury publishing, so it signals premium pricing instantly.
  • Gold appears only as a small diamond and two rules, which is elegant without any 3D effects.

Watch-outs

  • The fine hairlines need the dedicated small version for anything under 48 px (shown in the size test).
  • Needs clear space around the arch; don't squeeze it into tight corners.
B

Editorial

VanIndra set in Cormorant Garamond with Creations in gold italic, like a magazine masthead. The seal carries a single serif V inside a double ring.

Why it works

  • The most readable of the three at every size, from a pitch-deck cover to a 16 px tab icon.
  • Mixed case keeps the brand spelling VanIndra exactly as written everywhere else.
  • Warm and graceful, which suits family, lifestyle and festive creators.

Watch-outs

  • A V inside a circle is a familiar shape, so the icon alone is less ownable than A.
  • The italic Creations is best kept in gold or a lighter tone, never in a second loud colour.
C

Frame

A Didone V set inside a rounded 9:16 frame, the shape of a phone screen. The wordmark is geometric Jost with wide letter-spacing.

Why it works

  • Says "short-form video studio" before anyone reads a word.
  • Makes a strong app icon and favicon; the frame survives any size.
  • Modern and international, which fits B2B and global (USD) clients.

Watch-outs

  • Cooler and more corporate than A or B, with less warmth for family and lifestyle creators.
  • Rounded phone frames are common in tech; the Didone V carries most of the personality.

Recommendation

Direction A · Maison

It is the only option with a story built in. The arch is a 9:16 Reel frame and also the arched doorway of an Indian palace. The gold diamond above the V completes "Vi". It looks like a luxury house, so it supports higher prices in India and in USD markets. Direction B is the safe runner-up if the team prefers something quieter.
