# Logo Round 2: hand-built geometry concepts M1–M7 (14 Oct 2026)

> **Status:** for review only. Not approved; do not use on any public page.
> **Quick look:** open `00_Overview_M1-M7.jpg`. It shows every concept as an Instagram profile picture, a browser tab and a website header.
> **Full detail:** each folder has `sheet.jpg` (all versions), `in_use.png`, the vector files (`.svg`) and `rationale.md` (the idea and how it is built).

## How they were made
Seven AI design agents each drew one concept by hand, in code, from exact geometry: grids, true angles, measured stroke weights and optical corrections. No image generator was used, so every file is a clean vector that stays sharp from a 16 px browser tab to a hoarding. Each designer explored 36–90 variations and refined their concept 4–12 times.

Three independent AI judges then scored all seven:
1. **Creative director:** taste, originality and craft.
2. **Indian client:** a 4-lakh-follower creator and a D2C brand manager.
3. **Global client and production:** a US founder paying $2,000/month, plus a print/favicon check.

## Results

| # | Concept | The idea in one line | Judges (out of 10) | Verdict |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| M1 | Emerald Cut | A V cut like a 9:16 emerald | 4.5 / 4.5 / 4.5 | Dropped: looks like a plain V or a pen nib |
| M2 | Indra's Net | A V made of a strap of jewels and one thread | 4.5 / 5 / 4 | Dropped: reads as argyle, or as a tick when small |
| M3 | Threaded VI | V and I threaded through each other | 5 / 6 / 5.5 | Dropped: "VI" reads as "six" and as the Vi telecom brand |
| M4 | Jaali | The V as one point of a jaali star | 4.5 / 4 / 5 | Dropped: generic chevron, and the star is a sensitive symbol |
| **M5** | **Shirorekha** | **English letters hanging from a Hindi-style headline bar; the mark reads as V and व** | **6.5 / 7 / 7** | **Winner, picked by all 3 judges, but needs a final polish** |
| M6 | Signature | One pen stroke joining Van and Indra | 4 / 4.5 / 3.5 | Dropped: the I reads as J ("VanJndra") |
| M7 | Hook + Tick | A V cut into a gold hook and a tick | 4 / 5.5 / 6 | Dropped: looks like a fintech template |

**Why M5 won:** it is the only concept whose idea you can see without being told. Every Hindi letter hangs from one line, the *shirorekha*. VanIndra's English letters hang from the same line, and that "headline" is the first 3 seconds of a Reel, which is exactly what we sell. It is also the clearest of the seven at Instagram-profile size.

**What the judges said must still change:**
- The mark leans right and can look like a flag or a "T" in the round profile picture.
- The "NIN" letters look like a picket fence.
- The gold spaced-out "CREATIONS" line copies the rejected Round 1 style.
- The horizontal logo repeats the V ("V VANINDRA").
- Hindi readers must confirm the mark reads as व, not प or ब.

## What happens next: Round 3 (running now)
Four designers are working:
1. **Shirorekha, final:** M5 with every judge's fix.
2. **One Dot:** a bilingual "vanindra / वनिंद्रा" wordmark whose only gold detail is the dot that both spellings share over "ni".
3. **The Hinge:** a custom "VanIndra" where the n's stem becomes a tall I (Indra, the sky, rising over Van, the forest).
4. **Kolam Line:** one unbroken kolam-style line around a dot grid that forms a V (pan-Indian).

The best two get one more polish and judging round. Then they go to the team vote next to the 50 AI options.
