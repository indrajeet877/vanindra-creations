## What to look for (checklist)
1. **Elegant and premium?** Would you be proud to see it on a ₹25,000/month client's Reel or on a $2,000/month US client's invoice?
2. **Spelling 100% correct?**
3. **Still clear when tiny?** Imagine it as the Instagram profile picture and the browser-tab icon.
4. **Works in one colour?** (Emerald only, gold only, white only)
5. **Not cartoonish:** no 3D, no shiny gradients, no clip-art.
6. **Feels like us:** Indian roots, global class.
