## Step 1: The 5-Minute LLM "What To Post / What Not To Post" Mini-Audit
When Guggy or Sahil finds a high-potential 100K+ influencer (or when an influencer replies to our first DM), Indrajeet runs **Prompt #1** from `05_Content_Factory_and_LLM_Prompts/02_Master_LLM_Prompt_Pack.md`.

We send them a clean 1-page PDF / Notion page titled:
> **Custom Growth & Content Roadmap for @[TheirHandle] — Prepared by @[AgencyHandle]**

Inside that 1-page document:
1. **3 Things You Are Doing Right** (Builds goodwill and ego warmth).
2. **2 Content Leaks Costing You Views & Brand Deals** (e.g., slow first 3 seconds, mixing off-niche topics, missing on-screen hook text).
3. **"Stop Posting vs. Start Posting" Table:**
   - ❌ *What NOT to Post:* Unscripted 2-minute rants without visual changes; generic festival greetings on the main Reel grid.
   - ✅ *What TO Post:* 35–45 second myth-busters, 3-step listicles, and polarized niche opinions.
4. **3 Ready-to-Shoot Viral Reel Scripts (Word-for-Word)** tailored to their exact niche.

1. Sahil (`@yadavakarsh_07`) downloads the first 15–20 seconds of one of the influencer's recent underperforming Reels.
2. In 15–20 minutes (using CapCut Pro / Premiere / AI captions), Sahil adds:

   - Dynamic word-by-word highlighted captions

3. Send the 15-second video directly in WhatsApp/DM:
   > *"Hey [Name], our tech & editing lead Akarsh Yadav (`@yadavakarsh_07`) took the first 15 seconds of your recent Reel and upgraded the hook & pacing just to show you what your content looks like inside our pipeline. Watch the difference!"*
