## 1. How to Spot the "Ideal 100K+ Client" on Instagram (Qualification Checklist)

Before adding an influencer to `02_Target_Influencer_Lead_Sheet.csv`, check these 4 boxes:
- [ ] **Followers:** 1,00,000 to 5,00,000 (1 Lakh to 5 Lakh).
- [ ] **Monetization Signal:** They have an email/WhatsApp in bio, do brand collabs, sell a course/consultation, or run a clinic/coaching/business.
- [ ] **Visible Content Gap (Why they need us):**
  - *Gap Type A:* They haven't posted in 7–14 days (burnout / don't know what to post).
  - *Gap Type B:* Their followers are 150K+, but their recent Reels get only 3K–8K views (weak hooks / random topics).
  - *Gap Type C:* They talk directly to the camera with great knowledge, but have **no dynamic captions, weak thumbnails, or slow editing**.
- [ ] **Genuine Fit:** You actually like their niche and can see how our LLM scripts + Sahil's editing would double their retention.

## 2. The "Warm-Up" Protocol (48 Hours Before Sending a DM)

Never DM a 100K+ creator cold without showing up on their radar first:
1. **Day 1:** Follow them from the Agency Account AND from Guggy's (`@smitayadav_0901`) or Sahil's (`@yadavakarsh_07`) creator account.
2. **Day 1:** Leave **1 genuine, thoughtful comment** on their latest Reel (NOT emojis — write a real sentence about their topic so they see your notification).
3. **Day 2:** Reply to one of their Instagram Stories with a genuine reaction or compliment.
4. **Day 2 (2 hours later):** Send **Outreach Message #1** (see `02_DM_Email_and_Call_Scripts.md`) + simultaneously send the same note to their **Business Email / WhatsApp** in bio.
