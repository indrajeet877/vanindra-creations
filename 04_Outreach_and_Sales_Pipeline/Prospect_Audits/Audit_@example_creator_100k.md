## 4. 5 Ready-to-Shoot Viral Reel Concepts

### Concept 1 — "Roti vs. Rice" Myth-Buster
- **On-Screen Hook (0–3s):** `ROTI YA CHAWAL? 🤔 Galat jawab = belly fat`
- **Spoken Opening:** *"Agar aap bhi weight loss ke liye chawal band kar chuke ho — ruko, pehle ye suno."*
- **Core Value (15–35s):** Portion size and what you eat *with* it matter more than roti vs. rice. Show 2 plates side by side: the same calories, very different fullness.
- **CTA:** *"Comment 'PLATE' — main aapko apni weekly thali plan bhejta/bhejti hoon."*

### Concept 2 — 3 Gym Mistakes Delhi Beginners Make
- **On-Screen Hook:** `GYM KE PEHLE 30 DIN MEIN YE 3 GALTIYAN ❌`
- **Spoken Opening:** *"Pehle mahine mein log quit kyun karte hain? In 3 galtiyon ki wajah se."*
- **Core Value:** (1) Copying an advanced routine, (2) skipping protein at dinner, (3) zero sleep tracking. Numbered pop-ups with a 1-line fix each.
- **CTA:** *"Apne us dost ko tag karo jo kal se gym shuru kar raha hai."*

### Concept 3 — Office Lunch Swap (Saveable)
- **On-Screen Hook:** `OFFICE TIFFIN → HIGH PROTEIN in 5 MIN`
- **Spoken Opening:** *"Same tiffin, bas ye 2 cheezein add karo."*
- **Core Value:** Show a typical office tiffin, then add 2 cheap Indian protein add-ons (paneer, curd, chana, sprouts). Show prep in 3 fast cuts.
- **CTA:** *"Save karo — kal subah kaam aayega."*

### Concept 4 — Label Reading 101
- **On-Screen Hook:** `"HEALTHY" LIKHA HAI? PEHLE PEECHE PADHO 👀`
- **Spoken Opening:** *"Packet ke aage jo likha hai woh marketing hai — asli sach peeche hota hai."*
- **Core Value:** How to read sugar, serving size and the first 3 ingredients in 10 seconds. Use a generic product. Don't name or shame a brand.
- **CTA:** *"Comment 'LABEL' for my 1-page grocery checklist."*

### Concept 5 — Your Transformation Story (Trust Builder)
- **On-Screen Hook:** `MAIN BHI YAHIN SE SHURU HUA THA…`
- **Spoken Opening:** *"Ek time tha jab mujhe 10 push-ups bhi nahi hote the."*
- **Core Value:** A 20-second honest before/after story with 3 lessons. This is the Reel that turns viewers into followers, and later into clients and brand partners.
- **CTA:** *"Follow karo — next Reel mein meri exact Week 1 routine."*
