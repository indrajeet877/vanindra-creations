## 4. 5 Ready-to-Shoot Viral Reel Concepts

### Concept 1 — "1 Minute, 1 Trick" Series
- **On-Screen Hook (0–3s):** `सिर्फ 1 मिनट में ये ट्रिक याद हो जाएगी ✅` *(Sirf 1 minute mein ye trick yaad ho jaayegi)*
- **Spoken Opening:** *"Ye sawaal har saal exam mein aata hai — aur 80% bachche isme galti karte hain."* *(Use a % only if you have the data. Otherwise say "zyaadatar bachche".)*
- **Core Value (15–35s):** One topic, one memory trick, one worked example on a whiteboard or screen.
- **CTA:** *"Comment 'PART 2' — kal agla trick."*

### Concept 2 — Form Rejection Mistakes
- **On-Screen Hook:** `फॉर्म भरने से पहले ये 3 गलतियाँ मत करना ❌`
- **Spoken Opening:** *"Saal bhar padhai ki, aur form ek chhoti si galti se reject ho gaya?"*
- **Core Value:** 3 common mistakes (photo/signature size, category certificate, wrong date format), each with a 1-line fix.
- **CTA:** *"Isse apne us dost ko bhejo jo form bharne wala hai."*

### Concept 3 — Topper Daily Routine (Story)
- **On-Screen Hook:** `PRAYAGRAJ के कमरे से SELECTION तक — एक दिन का रूटीन`
- **Spoken Opening:** *"Room chhota tha, sapna bada. Ye tha mera roz ka routine."*
- **Core Value:** A realistic 6-slot study day (no "16 hours" myths), shot as quick cuts of the desk, notes and chai.
- **CTA:** *"Save karo aur kal se try karo."*

### Concept 4 — Myth vs. Fact: Career Choices
- **On-Screen Hook:** `MYTH: "सरकारी नौकरी नहीं मिली तो सब ख़त्म"`
- **Spoken Opening:** *"Ye baat ghar walon ko bhi sunao."*
- **Core Value:** 3 real alternative paths with what each needs. Keep it factual and avoid salary promises.
- **CTA:** *"Comment karo — aap kaunsa option chunoge?"*

### Concept 5 — Quick Quiz (Comment Engine)
- **On-Screen Hook:** `90% लोग इसका जवाब गलत देते हैं 🤯` *(Only if true for your audience; otherwise `क्या आप सही जवाब दे पाओगे?`)*
- **Spoken Opening:** *"Sirf 5 second hain aapke paas — answer comment karo."*
- **Core Value:** 1 MCQ, a 5-second countdown, then the answer with a 1-line explanation.
- **CTA:** *"Sahi jawab pin karunga/karungi — follow karo next quiz ke liye."*
